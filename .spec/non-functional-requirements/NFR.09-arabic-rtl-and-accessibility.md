## NFR.09: Arabic, RTL, and Accessibility

## Requirement

**ID:** NFR.09  
**Source:** SRS §§6–18  
**Title:** Arabic, RTL, and Accessibility  
**Priority:** Must Have  
**Status:** Approved

### Statement

The UberTib patient and staff experiences shall be Arabic-first, support right-to-left layout, and conform to WCAG 2.2 Level AA for applicable web and mobile interactions.

## Traceability

| Traces To | IDs | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Customer Needs | CN.03.1, CN.12.2 | Approved outcomes requiring this quality constraint. |
| Source Baseline | SRS §§6–18 | UberTib SRS v1.1 plus confirmed provisional defaults where identified. |

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] All production user journeys expose Arabic labels, state explanations, validation errors, and recovery guidance without untranslated internal codes.
- [ ] Status, error, protection, eligibility, and urgency are communicated using text or iconography in addition to color.
- [ ] Keyboard or assistive navigation, focus order, accessible names, text scaling, contrast, and screen-reader announcements pass the defined accessibility test suite.
