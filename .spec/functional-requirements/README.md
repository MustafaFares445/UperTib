## Governance

- Each requirement is independently testable and traces backward to one approved customer need and customer problem.
- The SRS identifier remains an alias; the dotted identifier is the implementation and test-planning identifier.
- Provisional policies and numeric defaults are versioned and may change prospectively after customer, clinical, legal, or operational review.
- A source fact may be corrected through an audited workflow, but computed outcomes and immutable snapshots are never directly overwritten.
