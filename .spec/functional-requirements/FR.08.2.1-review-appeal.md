## FR.08.2.1: Review Appeal

## Requirement

**ID:** FR.08.2.1  
**Authoritative SRS ID:** FR-026  
**Title:** Review Appeal  
**Priority:** Should Have  
**Status:** Approved

### Statement

The UberTib system should allow an authorized affected party to appeal a review’s eligibility or policy compliance without directly changing its rating content.

## Traceability

| Traces To | ID | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Customer Need | CN.08.2 | Patients, doctors, and the integrity team need UberTib to control review verification, publication, and appeal while keeping R separate from scientific classification. |
| Customer Problem | CP.08 | See the approved customer-problem baseline. |
| Authoritative SRS | FR-026 | Preserved requirement identity from UberTib SRS v1.1 |

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] An appeal records appellant, review, policy-grounded reason, supporting evidence, and submission time.
- [ ] Publication status changes require an authorized integrity decision with recorded findings and reason.
- [ ] Appeal handling cannot feed the review rating R into S/P/H/I or modify a scientific eligibility decision.
