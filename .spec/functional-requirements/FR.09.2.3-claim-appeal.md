## FR.09.2.3: Claim Appeal

## Requirement

**ID:** FR.09.2.3  
**Authoritative SRS ID:** FR-026  
**Title:** Claim Appeal  
**Priority:** Should Have  
**Status:** Approved

### Statement

The UberTib system should allow an authorized case party to appeal an eligible claim or dispute decision within the versioned appeal window.

## Traceability

| Traces To | ID | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Customer Need | CN.09.2 | Reviewers and case parties need UberTib to control evidence, deadlines, decisions, appeals, and external execution until a case closes with a documented reason. |
| Customer Problem | CP.09 | See the approved customer-problem baseline. |
| Authoritative SRS | FR-026 | Preserved requirement identity from UberTib SRS v1.1 |

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The appeal references the original decision, states grounds, includes evidence, and records submission time.
- [ ] Eligibility and deadline checks use the policy snapshot governing the original case or decision.
- [ ] The appeal is assigned to an authorized reviewer according to separation-of-duties rules and preserves the original decision.
