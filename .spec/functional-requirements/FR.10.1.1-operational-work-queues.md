## FR.10.1.1: Operational Work Queues

## Requirement

**ID:** FR.10.1.1  
**Authoritative SRS ID:** FR-029  
**Title:** Operational Work Queues  
**Priority:** Must Have  
**Status:** Approved

### Statement

The UberTib system shall provide scoped operational queues for verification, review, dispute, claim, financial follow-up, and exception work, ordered by policy priority and deadline.

## Traceability

| Traces To | ID | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Customer Need | CN.10.1 | UberTib staff need the platform to show verification, review, exception, and follow-up queues ordered by priority, deadline, and responsibility. |
| Customer Problem | CP.10 | See the approved customer-problem baseline. |
| Authoritative SRS | FR-029 | Preserved requirement identity from UberTib SRS v1.1 |

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Each work item shows type, case reference, current state, priority, due time, responsible scope, and blocking reason.
- [ ] Staff see and claim only items covered by their active permissions and organizational scope.
- [ ] Assignment, escalation, completion, reopening, and deadline breach are audited state transitions.
